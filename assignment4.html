Assignment 4 - OER

Your Privacy, Your Choice: Understanding Digital Footprint and Privacy Settings

1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this activity, the learner is expected to be able to:

  1. Define what is a Digital Footprint.
  2. Discern the two types of digital footprint and its examples.
  3. Understand what and how to use Privacy Settings.
  4. Encourage cybersecurity literacy among other online users, especially UPOU students.

2. Description

H5P Learning Object

A short 10-question situation-based quiz made through H5P, testing the learner’s knowledge in cybersecurity literacy through their understanding of Digital Footprint and Privacy Settings, accompanied by a 5-minute video animation.

3. Rationale

As a university that provides open and distance learning, the University of the Philippines — Open University (UPOU) provides most of its services online. From online learning, to transactions online, UPOU—especially its students—are bound to be vulnerable to breaches in their cybersecurity. With the increasing concern on digital literacy and the rise of Artificial Intelligence at an alarming rate, it has become more complicated for users of the digital world to protect themselves from the dangers it holds. As ICTDO Director, Assoc. Prof. Reynold Adrian Pugoy (2026) puts it, “I believe that UPOU students should be the most cyber security literate students in the country. Not only in the country but in the world because we are in the digital space, ’di ba. I mean our classes are online. The transactions are being done online.”1

The aim of this Open Educational Resource (OER) is to inform fellow UPOU students of the concept of digital footprint; alongside understanding the use of privacy settings in navigating digital platforms. Not only does it aim to define, but to further expand upon the capabilities and limitations through learning and testing one’s knowledge in cyber security literacy in today’s day and age.
